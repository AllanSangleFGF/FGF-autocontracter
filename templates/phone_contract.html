DEFINITION:

References to cell phones include cell phones or wireless Personal Digital Assistants (PDA) such as a Smartphone, Blackberry etc. that have a cell phone feature.

PURPOSE:

The purpose of the Cellular Telephone Use Policy is:

  • To provide standards and clarification for cell phone use.
  • To ensure safe work practices when considering the need to use a cell phone while driving or while performing other duties.
  • For team members using cell phones to make cost effective decisions regarding cell phone use and plan.

SCOPE:

This policy applies to all FGF Brands Inc. team members who use cellular phones for the business.

POLICY:

A. Cell Phone Use when a team member's phone is issued for business.

FGF Brands Inc. policy is taken directly from the Ontario Government Standards and Practices Commission (OGSPC) Advisory: The employer may allow team members to use the cellular telephones to talk to family members, make medical appointments, schedule service technicians, and confer with a child's school And take care of any of a variety of other matters which can only be accomplished during regular working hours without such usage being prohibited by law. In this situation, it would be less disruptive to permit team members to make such personal calls from the cellular telephones than to require a team member to take a break or leave from work to take care of personal matters. Personal telephone calls made during working hours from public employers' telephones should be brief and infrequent. Personal long-distance calls, even if the team member reimburses the company for the cost of such calls, may not be made on company telephones. If the company chooses a cellular telephone plan that includes long distance telephone calls at no extra charge the team members would be prohibited from using this service for personal calls. If the team member used this service, the team member would be receiving a financial gain or avoiding a financial detriment and would be in violation of the policy and the overcharge amount will be recovered through payroll for the same.

B. Cellular phone use in vehicles, at work sites, or while operating equipment

Currently provincial law prohibits the use of handheld cellular phones while driving except when they are being used with a hands-free accessory. The Ontario State Driving Policy only allows the use of a hands-free cellular phone while driving. Supervisors will have the authority to restrict or prohibit use of cellular phones at any time on the job when they consider such situations and use may present a safety hazard to the team member, co-worker, contractors, and/or to the general public and private property. Sending and/or reading text messages are prohibited while operating any vehicle.

C. Termination

When your employment with FGF Brands is terminated whether initiated by yourself or the company, you agree that the company reserves the right to delete all company or personal data with no obligation to provide you with a copy of your personal data and you waive your right to redress by the company for the same. Similarly, if you have ported your personal number to the company account with the company cell phone service provider, you have also waived your right to reclaim it back.

Work Sites

DO NOT engage in the use of a cellular phone while at any work site during which the operation of a cellular phone will be a distraction to the user and/or may create an unsafe work environment. Such work sites include but are not limited to: Operating lifts, skyjacks, maintenance and construction, operating or repairing energized equipment such as electrical panels, motors, or energized circuits. Such work sites must be secured or the cell phone used only by a team member while out of harm's way of such work environments.

GUIDELINES FOR USE OF CELLULAR PHONES:

Cellular phones are a tool to enhance team member productivity and provide a higher level of service to our customers. Cellular phone service is also more expensive than landline phones because of the airtime costs associated with cellular phone usage. Therefore, we ask that team members follow the guidelines below to help keep costs to a minimum.

1. Do not use your cellular phone to make a call if regular phone service is available. There is no charge for a local call made from a regular phone while local calls made or received on a cellular phone incur charges.

2. Avoid lengthy conversations on cellular phones. If you anticipate a call lasting more than a few minutes, try and arrange to call the person back from a regular phone.

3. Calls made to Long Distance numbers after the allotted number of minutes, the team member shall be liable for the costs involved. And this will be deducted from the team member's pay.

4. Team members in possession of Company supplied cell phones are expected to protect the equipment from loss, damage, or theft.

5. Team members should avoid illegal, disruptive messages and non-productive use of the cell phones.

6.  The Company prohibits the use of cell phones by team members while operating forklifts or other equipment. The prohibition includes receiving or placing calls/text messages/emails.
